# Averon Lab

Hey everyone

Hello everyone, I’m **Thatgurl**, founder of **Averon Lab**, a group of game modders and artists who work together on different modding projects.

I’m a game modder myself and I work on things like:

- Game mods and custom content
- Custom characters and cyberfaces
- 3D models and assets
- Textures, kits and graphics
- Custom teams and players
- Mod conversions and ports
- Gameplay modifications

I’m always open to working on different ideas, whether it’s something simple or a more specific custom project.
